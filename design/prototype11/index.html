Your board, your browser.

This whiteboard is a little playground for FewClicks Studios. Everything you add or move is saved only in this browser's local storage.

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
  • Nothing is uploaded: your photos never leave your device.
  • All fonts, images and code load from fewclicks.org itself.
  • Use Export to keep a copy, Reset to start over.

Tips

Drag to move, flick to toss · tap a pin to drop an item · double-click anything to edit it · right-click or long-press for the menu · scroll / pinch to zoom · Space + drag to pan · D pen (pressure-sensitive with a tablet), E eraser · S stickers · F flashlight · C snapshot · Del delete.

Nature: rain runs along the real shape of things and puts out fires. Wind pushes rain and pond water. Plants grow only when watered: drag one from Plants, then drag the watering can from Water over it. Snow piles up and freezes water. Paper burns. Fans blow, magnets pull metal, storm clouds strike. Rain pools on the ground for a few seconds and then soaks in; on cement (shovel bar) it stays. Dig a hole and let it rain to make a pond, or dig below the water table (8 m) and groundwater fills it. The ground is 30 m deep. Drag in a volcano and tap its crater to erupt. Everything is true to scale (1 m = 100 px): zoom in for small things.
